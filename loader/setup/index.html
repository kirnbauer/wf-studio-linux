Install or update Watch Face Studio

  1. Download Watch Face Studio for Windows from Samsung in your browser. Samsung shows its license agreement there.

  2. Choose the downloaded file (WatchFaceStudio_x.y.z.exe). It is checked for Samsung's digital signature before anything is installed.
